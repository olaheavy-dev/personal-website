# 0001: Positioning and audience

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Decided by:** the owner, in the design-flow grill session

## Context

The site must win a hiring manager in under a minute, and give consulting founders a clear path. The owner's story spans IoT, regulated banking, cross-border payments, edtech and production AI at HBK.

## Options

- AI-led, fintech as depth
- Chronological journey
- By capability
- Equal-weight generalist

## Decision

AI-led with fintech as depth; primary audience hiring managers for senior/staff AI and founding roles, consulting founders second. One line: **"I take products from idea to production, with AI built in."** (products first, AI as the multiplier; revised after the hero review). Primary call to action: **Email me**.

## Consequences

- Home leads with HBK (Advantage Insights platform, RAG, AI agents) and personal AI projects; banking and payments appear as proof of high-stakes production.
- Every page ends in a contact band; the hero carries the email CTA.
